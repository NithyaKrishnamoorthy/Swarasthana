Listening homework

Pick a film song you love and listen to one line at a time on repeat. Ask yourself: where does the singer slide instead of step? Which long notes start straight and only then start to shake? Where do they get softer instead of louder? Where does a note fall away at the end of a line?

Then come back and drill that technique here until it's automatic. Classical-style swarasthana accuracy (the other tab) is what makes these ornaments sound intentional rather than sloppy.
