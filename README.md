# Swarasthana

A daily Carnatic practice app for fixing swarasthanas (swara positions). It shows random swaras or short phrases in your chosen raga and shruti, plays a tanpura drone and a reference tone, and can check your pitch live with the microphone.

**Live:** https://nithyakrishnamoorthy.github.io/Swarasthana/

- Any shruti (by kattai), with fine tuning
- 16 ragas, all 12 swarasthanas, or a custom set
- Mandra, madhya and tara octaves
- Single-swara and phrase drills, "sing → check" or "listen → sing"
- Hands-free mode
- Mic pitch meter in cents (use headphones)
- Weaker swaras come up more often; daily goal, streak and 14-day history (saved in your browser)

**Film vocals tab:** drills for the expressive techniques of Tamil film singing: meend (glide), kan swara (grace note), delayed vibrato, swell & fade, murki, akaaram runs, scoop & fall-off, and a sangati ladder. Each exercise is an original phrase generated in a popular film raga, sung by a synthetic "aa" voice, and drawn as a pitch-and-loudness curve. With the mic on, your voice is traced over it and scored for pitch shape and dynamics.

It's a single static file (`index.html`) with no build step.
